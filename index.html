UK Cost of Living Watch

How have wages, tax, housing, food, energy and fuel changed for ordinary British households since 1990? Pick two years and compare, using official data only. Nothing is invented: gaps are shown as gaps.

Housing and mortgages

House price ÷ average annual earnings

UK average price (Land Registry UK HPI) divided by average weekly earnings × 52. Earnings before 2000 use the Bank of England composite series.

Mortgage rates

Standard variable rate (building societies 1990–94, Bank of England 1995 onward), 2-year fixed (1995 onward) and Bank Rate.

Average house price by region

Not yet integrated: average private rent in £, Council Tax levels, deposit requirements. See Data & gaps.

Food and shopping basket

Prices are ONS Retail Prices Index average prices for specified items. The basket quantities are an illustrative assumption, not official expenditure weights, and a year-on-year total only includes items observed in both years.

Energy and household bills

Typical annual bill (£, official, from 2010)

DESNZ modelled bills for a fixed consumption (3,600 kWh electricity, 13,600 kWh gas), cash terms. These are not actual household spend.

Fuel price index since 1990

ONS CPI electricity, gas and other fuels (2015 = 100). An index, not a bill.

Water and broadband/telecoms bills are not yet integrated. £ energy bills before 2010 are not published in the DESNZ tables; the compare tool uses a labelled index-scaled estimate there.

Transport and motoring

Petrol and diesel (£ per litre)

Prices indices (2015 = 100)

ONS CPI: new cars (quality-adjusted), rail fares, motor insurance (from 2015 in this dataset).

Vehicle categories (small, family, mid-range, van, luxury), purchase prices in £, insurance premiums, MOT, servicing, tyres and Vehicle Excise Duty rates are not yet integrated. Only pump prices and ONS price indices are available. See Data & gaps.
